Dyuti default wallpapers — attribution
=======================================

The shipped Dyuti desktop wallpapers are derived from KDE Plasma community
wallpapers (package: plasma-workspace-wallpapers), recomposited with a small
"Dyuti" wordmark in the lower-right corner. The underlying artwork and its
licence are credited below; the derivative works are redistributed under the
same terms.

dyuti-dark.png  (dark-theme desktop + lock wallpaper)
  Base: "MilkyWay" (KDE Plasma wallpaper)
  Licence: CC-BY-SA-4.0  (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/)

Additional wallpapers available in the picker are provided unmodified by the
plasma-workspace-wallpapers and ubuntu-wallpapers packages under their own
licences.

The abstract "Dyuti" vector wallpapers (dyuti-default.svg, dyuti-light.svg,
dyuti-lockscreen.svg, dyuti-bloom.svg) are original Dyuti artwork.
